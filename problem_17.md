Hallucination Stress Test

Theme D- Reliability. Hallucination and Safety

The Problem
Design 10 trap questions (false premises, fake references, nonexistent facts) and a
prompt that makes the model resist them.

Your Prototype Must Show
Before/after hallucination rate on your own test set.

Stretch Challenge (mandatory)
Build an automated harness with at least 20 trap questions; report hallucination rate
across 3 prompt versions and 2 models.

Every Team Must Also
Deliver a working prototype that runs on a new, unseen input.
Measure results: a labelled set of 10+ cases and one metric, first version vs final version.
Combine at least two prompting techniques and show a side-by-side comparison in the demo.
Build guardrail into the app (invalid output, off-topic input, refusals)
Keep timestamped prompt history (Git or doc)
